## 1. Academic & Medical Education Disclaimer

### Important Notice
The MedVigyaan platform is designed and operated **exclusively for medical education, academic discourse, student collaboration, and case-study peer reviews**.

1. **Not a Clinical Diagnostic Tool:** The platform and its content do **not** constitute medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or clinical prognosis.
2. **Not an Electronic Health Record (EHR):** The platform is not certified as an Electronic Medical Record (EMR) or EHR system under HIPAA, GDPR-Health, or national healthcare data regulations.
3. **No Emergency Services:** The platform must **never** be used for emergency medical situations or real-time patient triage. In the event of a medical emergency, immediately contact local emergency healthcare authorities.
4. **De-Identification of Clinical Case Discussions:** When discussing real-world clinical observations or medical literature cases in threads or blogs, members are strictly required to ensure that all patient identifiers (names, dates of birth, MRNs, recognizable images) are completely removed or anonymized in compliance with medical privacy laws.

## 2. Responsible Security Disclosure Policy

We deeply appreciate and encourage security researchers and community members who help protect The MedVigyaan platform by discovering and responsibly reporting security vulnerabilities.

### Guidelines for Responsible Reporting
If you believe you have discovered a potential security vulnerability:
1. **Private Reporting:** Please report the vulnerability privately via direct message to the maintainer on LinkedIn: [Deekshith Vodela](https://www.linkedin.com/in/deekshithvodela/).
2. **Do Not Exploit:** Do not attempt to access, modify, or destroy user data, interrupt service availability (e.g. DoS / DDoS), or execute destructive payloads against production servers.
3. **Coordinated Disclosure:** Allow reasonable time (minimum 30 days) for the maintainer to investigate, reproduce, and deploy a remediation before disclosing details publicly.
4. **Scope:** Vulnerability disclosures are welcomed across authentication bypasses, privilege escalation, cross-site scripting (XSS), injection attacks, and privacy leak vectors.
